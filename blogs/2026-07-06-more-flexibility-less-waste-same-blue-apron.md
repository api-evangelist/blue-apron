Starting summer 2026, ingredients for each recipe will no longer be grouped together in individual recipe bags. Instead, ingredients will arrive loose in the box. To make unpacking and cooking easier, we've updated our recipe cards with ingredient checklists so you can quickly confirm everything is there and gather what you need when it's time to cook.
